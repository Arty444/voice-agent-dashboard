Privacy Policy

McHugh Jiu Jitsu · Last updated June 2026

McHugh Jiu Jitsu ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us by phone or text, or provide your information to us, we may collect your name, phone number, and the content of your messages, along with details needed to schedule a class (such as a child's age for program placement).

How we use your information

We use your information solely to respond to and assist you — for example, to schedule or reschedule a free trial class, answer questions about our programs, schedule, and pricing, and follow up on your inquiry.

SMS / Text Messaging

By contacting McHugh Jiu Jitsu by phone or by providing your phone number, you consent to receive text messages from us related to your inquiry, such as scheduling a free trial class and answering your questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

We do not share, sell, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number, opt-in data, or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with service providers strictly as needed to operate our messaging and scheduling (for example, our phone/SMS provider), and never with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (856) 242-8626.
